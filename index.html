(02) Profile

About

A brand system is an interface waiting to be built.

I'm Aurora, a brand and visual designer based in Madrid with around four years of experience in corporate identity, digital communication, editorial design and audiovisual content.

Building brand guidelines taught me that consistency is a form of usability: a clear system helps people find their way, whether on a printed report or on a screen. Working on UX prototypes for an internal reservation platform alongside a development team made me want to understand the other side of the handoff.

In 2026 I completed the Full-Stack Web Development Bootcamp at Neoland. I now design and build interfaces where visual systems, usability and implementation are considered together, and I'm looking for roles in frontend development, UI engineering and UX/UI design.

Design & frontend development · Personal · 2026

This Portfolio

Role
Art direction and design; development with an AI-assisted workflow
Context
Personal project
Year
2026
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5.3, Git & GitHub

Context & objective

A portfolio that connects my brand and visual design background with the way I now build interfaces, designed and coded as a single, coherent system.

Approach

An editorial art direction inspired by brand guidelines: an expanded variable typeface used across its full weight range, one accent colour, visible grid lines and numbered sections. The site is a static, single-page build with semantic HTML, custom CSS on top of Bootstrap’s grid and components, and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript.

Designed by me, developed with an AI-assisted workflow: I defined the art direction, content and requirements, and reviewed and tested the code, an approach I practised throughout the Neoland bootcamp.

Details

  • Light & dark mode
  • Keyboard navigation
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Colour contrast checked against WCAG AA
  • Structured data & Open Graph
  • Responsive from 320 px
